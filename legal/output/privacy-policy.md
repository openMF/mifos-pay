# Privacy Policy — Mifos Pay

**Effective date:** June 6, 2026
**Last updated:** June 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mifos Initiative ("we", "us", or "our") handles
information in the **Mifos Pay** application (`org.mifospay`).

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

Mifos Pay stores data you enter directly — such as loan details, bill reminders,
and financial calculations — **locally on your device only**. This data is never
transmitted to our servers or any third party.

### Information Collected Automatically

Mifos Pay does not collect any analytics, crash reports, device identifiers, or
usage data. There is no account creation, no login, and no backend server.

### Information We Do NOT Collect

- No name, email address, or contact information
- No location data
- No payment or financial account credentials
- No device identifiers or advertising IDs
- No usage analytics or behavioural tracking

---

## 2. How We Use Information

All data entered into Mifos Pay is used solely to provide the features of the
application on your device. We have no access to this data.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored using your device's local storage mechanisms. It is protected
by your device's built-in security (screen lock, device encryption). We recommend
keeping your device software up to date to ensure the latest security protections
are applied.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

Mifos Pay uses the following third-party data sources:

- **World Bank Open Data** (worldbank.org) — publicly available economic indicators.
  No personal data is sent to World Bank.
- **FRED API** (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis) — publicly available interest
  rate data. No personal data is sent to FRED.

These services are read-only. Mifos Pay fetches publicly available data from them;
no data about you is transmitted.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Mifos Pay is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in
your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## 6. Your Rights

Because Mifos Pay does not collect or store personal data on any server, there is
no personal data for us to access, export, or delete on your behalf.

If you wish to remove all data associated with Mifos Pay, uninstall the application
from your device. This permanently deletes all locally stored data.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the
top of this page indicates when the most recent changes were made. Continued use of
Mifos Pay after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
